Things to Do in the Valley

Explore the valley at your own pace, in every season.

Cross-country skiing

Explore the Methow Trails network, with ski-in, ski-out access from Aspen House when trails are open. Check current conditions at Methow Trails.

Hiking & walking

Explore valley walks and mountain trails. Check seasonal access and conditions before heading out.

Mountain biking & cycling

Discover the valley’s trails and scenic roads. Check trail access and seasonal restrictions.

Snowshoeing & winter activities

Enjoy winter scenery on designated snowshoe routes and other winter trails.

Food, coffee & local shops

Explore Mazama, Winthrop and Twisp for local food, coffee and shops.

Scenic drives & day trips

Explore the Methow Valley and surrounding mountain scenery. Check road closures, especially in winter.

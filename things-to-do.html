Mazama · Methow Valley

Things to Do in the Valley

Explore the valley at your own pace, in every season.

Choose an activity below for specific outings. Distances are round trip unless noted. Check seasonal closures, trail passes and current conditions before setting out.

Cross-country skiing · Winter

Mazama Nordic trails

Start from Aspen House when grooming and snow allow. The valley has over 200 km of groomed trails for classic and skate skiing.

Community Trail

Plan an out-and-back toward the suspension bridge; choose your turnaround using the winter trail map.

Guide & visitor information ↗

Sun Mountain & Cub Creek

Explore additional Nordic areas for variety and more challenging terrain. Check passes, grooming and trail difficulty before leaving.

Hiking & walking · Spring to fall

Tawlks-Foster Suspension Bridge

Easy, 2 miles round trip from the Suspension Bridge Trailhead; a riverside outing with a picnic stop.

Guide & visitor information ↗

Goat Wall Trail

Moderate, 4.4 miles round trip and 1,260 feet of climbing; sweeping valley views.

Guide & visitor information ↗

Goat Peak Lookout

Strenuous, approximately 4.4 miles round trip. Check rough forest-road access and snow before driving.

Guide & visitor information ↗

Cutthroat Lake

Approximately 4 miles round trip to an alpine lake; forest pass required.

Guide & visitor information ↗

Falls Creek Falls

The first waterfall is a quarter mile from the trailhead on pavement; the longer route is 2.6 miles round trip.

Guide & visitor information ↗

Rainy Lake

A gentle paved route, 2 miles round trip, to a mountain lake. Seasonal highway access.

Guide & visitor information ↗

Mazama to Suspension Bridge

Easy rolling ride of about 5 miles round trip from Mazama Junction.

Brown’s Farm to Mazama

Beginner-friendly doubletrack, about 12 miles round trip. Check e-bike rules.

Buck Mountain

Intermediate 14-mile loop near Winthrop, with open-country views.

A varied network of singletrack for different skill levels.

Short paved approach to spectacular mountain views; uneven pavement in places. Highway 20 closes seasonally.

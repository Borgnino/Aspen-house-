The house
A place to slow down

Come home to the Methow.

Set among the trees in Mazama, Aspen House pairs contemporary architecture with warm wood ceilings, expansive windows, colorful artwork and inviting spaces to gather. Spend the day outdoors, then settle in by the gas stove or share a meal around the dining table.

A well-equipped kitchen, separate TV lounge and peaceful bedrooms make it easy to feel at home.
